Tilt&Tell

Phone on your forehead. Friends give clues. Tilt down when you get it.

How to play

  1. Pick who's playing. Everyone for themselves, teams, or a quick round with no scores.
  2. Choose categories. Mix as many as you want. Words come in a fresh random order every time.
  3. Hold the phone on your forehead, screen facing your friends. Don't peek!
  4. Friends give clues. They can describe, act, sing, or do impressions. They just can't say the word.
  5. Guess as many as you can before time runs out.

Ways to score

↕Swipe up or downGot it

🎤Say “Got it”or say the answer
🎤Say “Pass”or “Skip”

Unlock more decks

Some decks start locked. You earn a ⭐ star for every word you get right. Spend stars on the category screen to open new decks.

Voice works on most phones but depends on your browser. Turn it on in Settings. On iPhone, tap Allow when asked about motion so tilting works.

After each turn you can tap any word to fix the score, in case a tilt or a voice command got it wrong.
