# Tilt & Tell release notes

## 1.4.0
- Hand waves: turn on "Hand wave (camera)" in Settings, then wave across the front camera while the phone is on your forehead. Up or down is Got it, side to side is Pass. The flip switch swaps them. The camera picture never leaves the phone, and a 👋 shows while it's on.
- 24 new categories (60 in all): Fruits & Veggies, Desserts & Sweets, Breakfast & Drinks, Bugs & Birds, Farm & Pets, Plants & Flowers, Books of the Bible, Faith Words, Around the House, Clothes & Accessories, Tools & Building, Superpowers & Spy Gear, Planets & Space, Summer Fun, Winter Wonderland, Fall & Thanksgiving, Spring & Easter, Hobbies & Crafts, Dance Moves, Animal Charades, Around Town, History & Heroes, Colors & Shapes, and Baby Animals.
- New "One category or Mix several?" question before you pick. One category starts as soon as you tap it.
- NEW badges and quick section buttons on the category screen.
- Swipes count as soon as your finger moves far enough, so fast flicks don't get missed.

## 1.3.0
- Golden cards: now and then a gold card shows up worth double points (can be turned off in Settings).
- Hot streaks: get 3 in a row and a flame counter appears; each answer in a streak sounds a little higher.
- Final 10 seconds: the screen pulses and the timer beats so everyone feels the rush.
- End-of-game awards: Lightning Brain, On Fire, Golden Touch, and Word Explorer.
- Turn results show your best streak and fastest guess, with an animated score.
- Smarter voice: understands joined or split words (Hulkbuster / Hulk buster), plurals, and near-misses on longer words. On Android 13+ the app also tells the phone which word to listen for.
- Bigger Got it button, new card animations, and a big category picture behind each word.
- New setting to flip swipe directions.

## 1.2.0
- Android app (APK) you can install straight from the public Updates page.
- Check for updates in Settings. The app also checks when it opens and offers a one-tap Update.
- In the Android app, voice uses the phone's own speech engine.

## 1.1.0
- One word per card by default (setting to allow longer cards). Added hundreds of new one-word cards.
- Unlockable decks: earn a star for every word you get right and spend stars to open new decks. Leaders can unlock all in Settings.
- Pass and Got it are now nearly instant.

## 1.0.0
- First release: 36 categories, about 3,800 words.
- Each player, teams, or quick play.
- Tilt, swipe, on-screen buttons, and voice controls.
- Custom decks, offline play, installable on iPhone and Android.
